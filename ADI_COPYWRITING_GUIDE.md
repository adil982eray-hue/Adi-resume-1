## ✍️ WRITING TIPS FOR CONVERSION

### Do's ✅
- Use specific numbers (not "many" but "150%")
- Focus on benefits, not features
- Use power words: "Transform," "Accelerate," "Scale," "Dominate"
- Keep sentences short and scannable
- Use active voice ("We helped you grow" not "Growth was achieved")
- Address pain points directly
- Include social proof/testimonials
- Create sense of urgency ("Limited spots available")
- Always include clear CTA

### Don'ts ❌
- Avoid jargon that confuses
- Don't oversell or make unrealistic promises
- Avoid "we" overuse (focus on client benefits)
- Don't use passive voice
- Avoid vague terms ("better," "improved," "enhanced")
- Don't hide pricing
- Don't make CTAs too subtle
- Avoid clutter and too much information
